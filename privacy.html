Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations on our tools run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. No data leaves your device.

2. Local Storage

Some tools use your browser's localStorage to save your inputs for convenience. This data stays on your computer and is never sent to our servers. You can clear it anytime through your browser settings.

3. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. We may use essential cookies for site functionality only. Third-party advertisers (if any) may use cookies per their own policies.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Plausible or GoatCounter) that do not use cookies or track individual users across sites.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email privacy@coveragecheckr.com.
