🏠 Home Insurance Valuator

Estimate your home's replacement cost and inventory your belongings. Avoid the $100k underinsurance mistake.

About This Tool

Estimate your home replacement cost and check if your dwelling coverage is sufficient. Most homeowners are underinsured by $100,000 or more because they confuse market value with replacement cost.

How It Works

Enter your home square footage, construction cost per square foot, home age, current dwelling coverage, flood zone status, earthquake zone, and personal property value. The calculator estimates replacement cost and identifies gaps.

Why It Matters

If your home burns down and you are insured for $200,000 but replacement costs $350,000, you pay $150,000 out of pocket. Standard policies also exclude flood and earthquake. This calculator finds those gaps.
