💰 Life Insurance Needs Estimator

Calculate how much life insurance you actually need based on income, debts, and family obligations.

About This Tool

Calculate how much life insurance you actually need based on your income, debts, family obligations, and existing coverage. Most people with group life through work are severely underinsured.

How It Works

Enter your annual income, spouse income, mortgage balance, other debts, number of children, college savings goals, current savings, and existing life insurance. The calculator uses the DIME method (Debt, Income, Mortgage, Education) to recommend a coverage amount.

Why It Matters

Group life insurance ends when you leave your job. If you die with only $50,000 in coverage and a $300,000 mortgage, your family loses the house. This calculator shows the real number.
