🚗 Auto Coverage Optimizer

Enter your car's details and driving habits. Get recommendations on deductibles and coverage types.

About This Tool

Optimize your auto insurance based on your vehicle value, age, driving habits, and current coverage. Find out if you are over-paying or dangerously under-covered.

How It Works

Enter your vehicle value, age, annual mileage, current liability limits, deductible, and coverage types. The calculator recommends specific changes like raising deductibles, dropping comprehensive on old cars, or adding uninsured motorist coverage.

Why It Matters

State minimum liability limits are dangerously low. A single accident with injuries can exceed $250,000 in damages. This calculator tells you if your coverage matches your risk.
